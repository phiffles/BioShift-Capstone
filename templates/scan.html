Add the Legacy Photo ?
What counts as "legacy"?
An older photo of the person — e.g. an ID card or passport scan, a childhood photo, or a faded print. It doesn't need to be recent, just clear enough to show the face.

Upload or capture one or more historical/legacy photos of the person (up to 5). Each will be age-progressed and compared against the present photo — the best match is used, and the rest are kept for reference.

Biometric Data Consent
Required before processing

By proceeding, you acknowledge and consent to the following:

  • Data collected: Facial images (legacy photo and present photo) and derived biometric data (face embeddings, age-progressed images).
  • Purpose: Age-invariant identity verification — comparing an age-progressed version of the legacy photo against the present photo to assess identity similarity.
  • Processing: Images are processed locally on this device using AI models (SAM age progression, InsightFace face verification). No data is transmitted to external servers.
  • Storage: All photos and results are stored locally in the application database. Data may be reviewed by authorized administrators through the discrepancy queue.
  • Retention: Data is retained until manually deleted by an administrator.
